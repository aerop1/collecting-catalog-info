Collecting Catalog

Collecting Catalog is an app for Android and Windows that helps you catalog any kind of collection: shells, coins, stamps, CDs, or anything else. You describe your collection with your own fields, and you keep a record of every item.

What the app does

  • Create collections from ready-made templates (shells, coins, stamps, CDs) or build your own template with your own fields.
  • Add photos, documents, and web links to every item.
  • Export a collection as a CSV table or as a PDF with one page per item.
  • Keep your data on your devices, and sync it between your Android phone and your Windows computer.

Google Drive sync

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app uses one permission: access to a hidden, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. The app uses this folder only to sync your catalog between your own devices. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Google Drive.

Read the Privacy Policy to see exactly how your data is handled.
