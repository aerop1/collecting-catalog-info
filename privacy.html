Collecting Catalog - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who we are

Collecting Catalog is an app for Android and Windows, made by Quick Thumbs. Contact: u8012154951@id.gle

What data the app handles

The app stores your catalog data (your collections, items, field values, notes, photos, documents, and links) on your own device, in the private storage of the app. We do not run any server, and we do not collect this data. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.

Google user data

If you choose to sign in with your Google Account, the app asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This permission gives the app access only to a hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive. The app cannot see, read, or change any other file in your Google Drive.

How the app uses this access: the app writes copies of your catalog data and your photos and files into this hidden folder, and it reads them back on your other devices. The only purpose is to keep your own catalog in sync between your own devices.

How the data is stored: the data stays in your own Google Drive. The sign-in information (tokens) that the app needs to talk to Google is stored only on your device.

Sharing: we do not share, sell, or transfer your Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use analytics or tracking.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can use the app without signing in with Google. Then no data leaves your device.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can delete the hidden app data in Google Drive under Settings, Manage apps.
  • You can delete all local data by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
